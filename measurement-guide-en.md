# Measurement Guide

*Original English text of the measurement guide by Ioanna Custom Sewing, LLC ([orthodox-attire.com/measurement-guide](https://orthodox-attire.com/measurement-guide)).*

1\. **Rasso Length:** Measure from the base of the back of your neck to where you would like the bottom of the rasso hem to fall.

1\. **Cassock Length:** Measure from the base of the back of your neck to where you would like the bottom of the cassock hem to fall.

2\. **Vest Length:** Measure from the base of the back of your neck where the base of your collar sits to where you would like the bottom of the vest hem to fall.

3\. **Neck:** Measure around your neck.

4\. **Yoke:** Measure straight across your back from shoulder bone to shoulder bone.

5\. **Arm Length:** With the arm straight and held perpendicular to the body, measure from the center base of the back of the neck, across the shoulder, and along the arm to the wrist.

5\. **Rasso Sleeve Length:** With the arm straight and held perpendicular to the body, measure from the center base of the back of the neck, across the shoulder, and along the arm to the desired rasso sleeve length.

5\. **Cassock Sleeve Length:** With the arm straight and held perpendicular to the body, measure from the center base of the back of the neck, across the shoulder, and along the arm to the desired cassock sleeve length.

6\. **Shoulder:** Measure from the side of your neck where the base of your collar sits to your shoulder bone arm hinge.

7\. **Bicep:** Measure around the fullest part of the bicep with your arm by your side.

8\. **Wrist:** Measure around your wrist at the bone.

9\. **Chest:** Measure around your chest at its fullest part under the armpits.

10\. **Stomach/Waist:** Measure around the fullest part of the stomach or right below your bottom ribs, whichever is greater.

11\. **Waistband:** Measure around your body at the height were you would wear your pants.

12\. **Half Back:** Measure from the base of the back of the neck down your spine to where you measured your waist or where you would wear your pants. (This is where the buttons and the top of the pockets on the side and the ties will be placed.)

13\. **Hips/Seat:** Measure around the fullest part of your buttocks.

14\. **Outseam:** Measure from where you measured your waist along the side of your leg to where you would like the bottom of the pant hem to fall.

15\. **Head Perimeter:** Measure around your head where you wear your skoufi or skoufo.
